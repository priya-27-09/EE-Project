# Municipal Stormwater Drain Desilting Charter 1790179192

Unique Verification Code: DRAIN_DESILT_1790179192

## Section 1: Pre-Monsoon Desilting Schedule
All roadside micro-drains and arterial stormwater channels must be fully desilted by May 31st each year.

## Section 2: Waterlogging Emergency Redressal
Severe waterlogging complaints on major road junctions must be deployed with heavy dewatering pumps within 2 hours.
