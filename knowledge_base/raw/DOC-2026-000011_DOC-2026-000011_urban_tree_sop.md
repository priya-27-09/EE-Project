## Section 1: Fallen Tree Clearance
Fallen trees obstructing arterial roadways must be cleared by the Disaster Management Cell within 4 hours.

## Section 2: Pruning Standards
Overhanging dangerous tree branches near high-tension electrical wires must be pruned within 24 hours of notification.
