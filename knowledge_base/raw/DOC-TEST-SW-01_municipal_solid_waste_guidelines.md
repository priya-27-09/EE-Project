# Municipal Solid Waste Management Operational Guidelines

**Source:** Ministry of Housing and Urban Affairs (CPHEEO 2016 Municipal Solid Waste Rules)  
**Source URL:** https://mohua.gov.in/upload/uploadfiles/files/Part2.pdf  
**Department:** Sanitation  
**Document ID:** DOC-2026-000001  
**Version:** 1.0  

## Section 1: Overview and Scope
These operational guidelines prescribe municipal standards for door-to-door collection, segregation, transportation, and prompt redressal of municipal solid waste complaints in urban local bodies. Proper municipal sanitation is essential for public health, disease prevention, and urban cleanliness.

## Section 2: Waste Segregation Mandate
All urban households, commercial establishments, and institutions must segregate solid waste into three distinct streams at source:
1. **Bio-degradable (Wet Waste):** Kitchen waste, vegetable peels, leftover food, garden leaves, and organic matter deposited in green bins.
2. **Non-Biodegradable (Dry Waste):** Plastic, paper, cardboard, metal cans, glass, and packaging materials deposited in blue bins.
3. **Domestic Hazardous Waste:** Expired medications, paint cans, discarded batteries, tube lights, and sanitary waste deposited in red or designated safe receptacles.

Mixed waste deposition is prohibited. Sanitation workers are authorized to refuse unsegregated waste after providing two written notices to the occupier.

## Section 3: Collection Timings and Frequencies
1. **Residential Areas:** Door-to-door collection must be performed daily between 06:30 AM and 11:30 AM using compartmentalized primary collection vehicles.
2. **Commercial Zones:** Primary collection must occur twice daily: morning between 07:00 AM and 09:30 AM, and evening between 08:00 PM and 10:30 PM.
3. **Markets and Fish/Meat Vending Zones:** Continuous clearance with designated compactor trucks twice daily.

## Section 4: Garbage Overflow and Complaint Redressal SLA
When a citizen reports a garbage overflow or uncleared community dustbin through the civic grievance portal:
- **First Response & Inspection:** The designated Ward Sanitary Inspector must inspect the site within 4 hours of complaint logging.
- **Clearance & Disposal:** Garbage overflow must be completely cleared, loaded onto hydraulic compactor trucks, and transported to the designated processing facility within **12 hours** of complaint registration.
- **Disinfection Standard:** Immediately after clearing accumulated solid waste, the surface must be disinfected using bleaching powder (calcium hypochlorite) or lime wash to mitigate odor, fly breeding, and pathogens.
- **Evidence Verification:** The sanitary supervisor must capture geotagged before-and-after photographic evidence to record official resolution in the portal.

## Section 5: Penalties for Illegal Dumping
Unauthorized disposal of construction debris, bulk industrial waste, or hazardous materials on public roads, storm drains, or open plots is strictly prohibited. Offenders are subject to administrative spot fines:
- Residential violators: INR 500 for first violation, INR 1,000 for repeated offenses.
- Commercial establishments: INR 5,000 for first violation, escalating to municipal trade license suspension for chronic illegal dumping.
