# Standard Operating Procedure for Urban Road Repair and Pothole Maintenance

**Source:** Indian Roads Congress (IRC:SP:98-2020 Guidelines on Maintenance of City Roads)  
**Source URL:** https://irc.nic.in/guidelines-urban-roads  
**Department:** Roads & Transport  
**Document ID:** DOC-2026-000002  
**Version:** 1.0  

## Section 1: Purpose and Applicability
This Standard Operating Procedure governs the systematic inspection, priority rating, and engineering execution of pothole repairs, asphalt patching, and road surface restoration across municipal arterial roads, sub-arterials, and residential collector streets.

## Section 2: Pothole Classification and Severity Matrix
Road surface depressions and potholes are classified into three severity levels to dictate repair urgency:
1. **Low Severity (Category C):** Potholes with depth less than 25 mm (1 inch) and diameter under 300 mm. Repair required during routine weekly maintenance rounds.
2. **Medium Severity (Category B):** Potholes with depth between 25 mm and 50 mm, or diameter up to 600 mm on residential roads. Repair mandated within **48 hours**.
3. **High Severity (Category A - Hazardous):** Potholes with depth exceeding 50 mm (2 inches), edges with sharp asphalt breakage, or any pothole situated on arterial roads, bus corridors, or bridge approaches. Mandatory repair window: **24 hours**.

## Section 3: Engineering Repair Methodology
All repairs must adhere to standard technical specifications rather than loose soil or rubble filling:
1. **Perimeter Marking and Excavation:** The defective area must be cut into a neat rectangular or square geometry using a diamond blade pavement saw or pneumatic jackhammer, extending at least 50 mm beyond visible cracks into sound pavement.
2. **Debris Removal & Base Preparation:** All loose aggregate, dirt, and water must be completely removed using compressed air or stiff wire brooms. If water is present, the hole must be dried before applying binder.
3. **Tack Coat Application:** Emulsified bitumen (Rapid Setting RS-1 or Medium Setting MS) must be applied uniformly to the vertical edges and floor of the cut at 0.25 to 0.35 kg/m².
4. **Patch Material Deposition:**
   - **Dry Season:** Hot Mix Bituminous Concrete (BM/BC) compacted in layers not exceeding 50 mm thickness.
   - **Wet / Monsoon Season:** Approved cold-mix asphalt formulations with anti-stripping additives (IRC:SP:100 compliant).
5. **Compaction Standard:** Mechanical compaction using a walk-behind vibrating roller or plate compactor to achieve at least 98% laboratory density flush with the adjacent road profile.

## Section 4: Safety Protocols During Road Works
- **Barricading:** High-visibility reflective orange cones or barricades must be positioned 30 meters ahead of the work zone during daytime repairs.
- **Night Work Illumination:** For night repairs, illuminated directional arrows and minimum 500-lumen mobile floodlights must be operational. Workers must wear fluorescent retroreflective vests.
- **Traffic Diversion:** At least one lane width (minimum 3.0 meters) must remain open for moving vehicular traffic unless a detour has been gazetted.

## Section 5: Complaint Resolution Service Level Agreement (SLA)
- Arterial & Bus Route Potholes: Resolved within **24 hours** of citizen notification.
- Residential Collector Streets: Resolved within **48 to 72 hours**.
- Temporary cold-patch repairs during torrential rain must be followed by permanent hot-mix restoration within 14 days of dry weather.
