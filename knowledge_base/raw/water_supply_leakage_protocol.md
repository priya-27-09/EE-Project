# Municipal Water Supply Network Leakage and Contamination Protocol

**Source:** Central Public Health and Environmental Engineering Organisation (CPHEEO Manual on Water Supply and Treatment)  
**Source URL:** https://cpheeo.gov.in/upload/uploadfiles/files/Water_Supply_Manual.pdf  
**Department:** Water Supply  
**Document ID:** DOC-2026-000003  
**Version:** 1.0  

## Section 1: Objective
This protocol establishes statutory operating procedures for detecting, isolating, excavating, repairing, and hydraulically testing municipal potable water pipeline leaks, bursts, and pressure losses in the civic distribution network.

## Section 2: Pipeline Leakage Categories and Response Timelines
1. **Critical Main Transmission Burst (Diameter > 300 mm):**
   - High-volume discharge causing road flooding or severe pressure loss across municipal distribution zones.
   - Sluice valve isolation required within **1 hour** of report.
   - Engineering repair and pipeline recharging must be completed within **8 to 12 hours**.
2. **Distribution Sub-Main Leakage (Diameter 100 mm to 300 mm):**
   - Repair turnaround window: **18 hours**.
3. **Consumer Service Connection Leakage (Ferrule / Saddle Cock Leak):**
   - Repair turnaround window: **24 hours** from complaint verification.

## Section 3: Isolation and Excavation Procedure
1. **Valve Isolation:** Turn off upstream isolation gate or sluice valves to de-pressurize the affected sector without starving adjacent hospital, school, or critical emergency facilities.
2. **Underground Utility Clearance:** Before motorized backhoe excavation, consult municipal GIS maps for intersecting high-voltage electrical cables, underground gas pipelines, and telecom fiber conduits. Manual trial pits must precede mechanical digging.
3. **Dewatering:** Submersible trash pumps must clear trench ponding before pipe cutting or mechanical repair sleeve installation.

## Section 4: Mechanical Repair Standards
- **Cast Iron / Ductile Iron Pipes:** Repaired using stainless steel full-circle repair clamps (AISI 304/316) with vulcanized EPDM rubber gaskets torqued to manufacturer specifications.
- **HDPE / MDPE Pipes:** Electrofusion couplers or mechanical compression fittings installed strictly by certified technicians.
- **Thrust Blocks:** Any newly installed bend or tee junction must be reinforced with concrete thrust blocks (minimum M20 mix) before restoring normal working pressure (typical 2.0 to 4.0 bar).

## Section 5: Water Quality and Disinfection Mandate
Whenever pipeline depressurization occurs, negative hydraulic pressure can suck contaminated groundwater into the main:
1. Following repair, the affected line must be flushed at a velocity not less than 0.75 m/s.
2. Super-chlorination with sodium hypochlorite at 20 mg/L residual chlorine for a 2-hour contact period.
3. Field test for free residual chlorine (minimum 0.2 mg/L at farthest consumer tap) and turbidity (< 1 NTU) before declaring water supply safe for human consumption.
