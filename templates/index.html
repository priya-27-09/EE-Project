How Civic Redressal Works

From civic report to verified field resolution in four transparent, accountable steps.

Step 1 📸

Upload Evidence & Location

Take a photo of the pothole, waste overflow, or broken street light. Automatic GPS pinpoints the municipal coordinates.

Step 2 🤖

AI Intake & Classification

Computer vision analyzes evidence while NLP processes your description in English or Tamil to determine canonical category and urgency.

Step 3 🏛️

Department Officer Dispatch

Routed automatically to the authorized department officer (Roads, Sanitation, Water, Electricity) with SLA tracking.

Step 4 ✅

Resolution & Verification

Officers submit completion photos. Visual verification confirms the fix before closing, and you receive instant updates.
